**Available materials:** Survey data and replication code.

[Article](https://doi.org/10.1093/sf/soag130) · [Data and replication code](https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/TDYS8)


#### Digital engagement and youth

**Associated publication:** Salza, G., & Samuel, R. (2026). Digital engagement and youth: A scoping review of opportunities, risks, and the role of socioeconomic resources. *Information, Communication & Society, 29*(9), 2756–2774.

**Available materials:** Dataset of included studies, Stata analysis code, and online appendix.
