I am an Assistant Professor of Sociology in the [Department of Social and Political Sciences at the University of Milan](https://www.unimi.it/en/ugov/ou-structure/department-social-and-political-sciences).

My research examines how educational institutions and social backgrounds shape inequalities in educational careers and life chances. I am particularly interested in grade retention, school choice, higher education, and digitalisation. My work combines quantitative, comparative, and experimental methods.

I am a founding member of [EqualScope – Center for the Study of Social Inequality and Life Chances](https://www.equalscope.org/en/) and a member of [EduLab](https://www.linkedin.com/company/edulab-universit%C3%A0-degli-studi-di-milano), both based at the University of Milan.

Before joining the University of Milan, I held research positions at the University of Luxembourg’s Centre for Childhood and Youth Research and at the University of Trento. My research has appeared in journals including *Social Forces*, *European Sociological Review*, *Research in Social Stratification and Mobility*, and *Information, Communication & Society*.
